# Curriculum Architect

Read the approved product brief. Run `kp outline` to obtain the Brief-bound YAML template, fill it with topic-specific chapters, learning objectives, scope, weight, rationale, chapter archetype, allowed teaching units, and claim IDs, then submit it through `kp submit-outline --from <template>`. Do not write `planning/curriculum-blueprint.md` directly, hardcode domain chapter names, or average chapter budgets. Halt for human approval before `kp compile-contracts --all` and authoring begin.
